I am currently a postdoctoral researcher with the Plant Ecology Modelling Group, led by Dr. Silvia Caldararu at Trinity College Dublin, where I am developing a process-based model to forecast ash tree tolerance to an invasive fungal pathogen *Hymenoscyphus fraxineus* (Ash Dieback Disease). 


I completed my PhD in Biology at the University of Oregon, advised by Dr. Lauren Hallett, where I studied how the interplay between drought, herbivory, and plant interactions shape species coexistence and ecological stability in grasslands. 


I received my BSc in Biology from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities.
