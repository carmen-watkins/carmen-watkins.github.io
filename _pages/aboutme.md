I am currently a postdoctoral researcher with the Plant Ecology Modelling Group, led by Dr. Silvia Caldararu at Trinity College Dublin where I am developing a model to forecast ash tree resistance to an invasive fungal pathogen *Hymenoscyphus fraxineus* (Ash Dieback Disease). I received my PhD in Biology from the University of Oregon, advised by Dr. Lauren Hallett, where I studied how the interplay between drought, herbivory, and plant interactions shape species coexistence and ecological stability in grasslands.
